                    Alabama at a glance: 5 and 10 years

                    Fixed periods that don't move with the slider. All manufacturing, private sector. "Local edge" is what's left of Alabama's job change after taking out the national trend and its mix of industries: how Alabama's plants did against the same industries nationwide.

                    Before and after the new incentives

                      Growth is shown per year so periods of different length compare fairly. The timing of a law lining up with a change doesn't show that the law caused it: interest rates, the 2020 recession and company decisions made years earlier all play a part.

                      Industry trends: 5 and 10 years

                      What each category includes

                      Every manufacturing industry BLS tracks sits in exactly one category and one industry group, so together they add up to all of manufacturing. The small numbers are NAICS industry codes, the federal system BLS uses to classify businesses.

                      Data sources

                      • BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages: jobs, establishments (plants and shops) and pay by county, state and industry, private sector, annual averages.
                      • BLS Consumer Price Index: used to put pay in today's dollars.
                      • Census Bureau County Business Patterns: plants by number of employees, county and state.
                      • Office of the Governor and Alabama Department of Revenue: Alabama incentive laws.
                      • Census Bureau metro area definitions (July 2023): which counties make up each metro area.
                      • us-atlas: county outlines from the Census Bureau.
