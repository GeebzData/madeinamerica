Who is landing the projects? By each state's own count

Every Southeast state's economic development agency publishes what it landed each year: how many projects, how many jobs companies committed to, and how much they promised to invest. This page puts those official figures side by side for 2021 to 2025, scaled by population so large and small states compare fairly. Alabama is highlighted.

Every project, job and dollar figure here comes from the states' own agencies. The only outside number is population, from the Census Bureau, used to put states on the same scale.

      Reading this fairly

      • These are announcements: jobs companies committed to and investment they promised. Some projects arrive late, shrink or are cancelled, and no state reports a five-year follow-up in the same release.
      • Alabama counts every new or expanding project it records, with or without incentives. Most peers count only projects their agency worked. Under the narrower rule Alabama's totals would be smaller, so where Alabama trails, the real gap is likely wider.
      • Georgia and Mississippi report state fiscal years ending June 30. Their 2025 column is July 2024 to June 2025, six months ahead of the calendar-year states.
      • Rounded figures from a release ("more than 34,000") are used as published and marked with a ~.
      • Per-resident figures divide by population estimated for each year from the Census Bureau's 2020 and 2025 counts. Investment is in each year's dollars, not adjusted for inflation.
