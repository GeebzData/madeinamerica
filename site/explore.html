        What each category includes

        Every manufacturing industry BLS tracks sits in exactly one category and one industry group, so together they add up to all of manufacturing. The small numbers are NAICS industry codes, the federal system BLS uses to classify businesses.

        Data sources

        • BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages: jobs, establishments (plants and shops) and pay by county, state and industry, private sector, annual averages.
        • BLS Current Employment Statistics: national manufacturing jobs since 1979 (via FRED).
        • Federal Reserve G.17 Industrial Production: manufacturing output (via FRED).
        • Census Construction Spending (FRED TLMFGCONS): manufacturing construction, as reported. The combined chart uses the inflation-adjusted series from the Reindustrialization page.
        • BLS Producer Price Index for new industrial building construction (FRED PCU236211236211): the building-cost deflator. Pay uses the Consumer Price Index instead.
        • BLS Consumer Price Index (FRED CPIAUCSL): used to put pay in today's dollars.
        • Census Bureau County Business Patterns: plants by number of employees.
        • us-atlas: county and state outlines from the Census Bureau.
