      Announcement trackers are not payroll jobs. The employment figures elsewhere on this page are BLS. Kentucky and Mississippi are outside the six-state Atlas/SACE set. The Southeast page compares Alabama with its peers on electrical employment, which is the battery-belt gap in the QCEW data.

      What each category includes

      Every manufacturing industry BLS tracks sits in exactly one category and one industry group, so together they add up to all of manufacturing. The small numbers are NAICS industry codes, the federal system BLS uses to classify businesses.

      Data sources

      • Census Bureau Construction Spending: private factory construction by industry, monthly, seasonally adjusted annual rate. Published nationally only.
      • BLS Producer Price Index for new industrial building construction (FRED PCU236211236211): used to adjust construction spending for building costs. The dashed line on the chart is the unadjusted Census series.
      • Atlas Public Policy and the Southern Alliance for Clean Energy: announced EV investment, cancellations, and the Alabama end-of-2025 snapshot in the note below.
      • Federal Reserve industrial production, manufacturing (FRED IPMAN, 2017 = 100): the output index on the chart above, annual averages plus the latest month.
      • BLS Current Employment Statistics, manufacturing (FRED MANEMP): national factory jobs on that chart. State and industry jobs elsewhere on the page are QCEW.
      • Bureau of Economic Analysis, GDP by state: real manufacturing GDP in chained 2017 dollars. 2025 is the latest year BEA has published here.
      • BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages: establishments (plants and shops) and jobs by state and industry, private sector, annual averages.
      • us-atlas: state outlines from the Census Bureau.
