    What each industry includes

    The industry chart on this page uses the Bureau of Economic Analysis's 19 manufacturing industries, which are a little broader than the industry groups on the other pages. Each is listed with its NAICS codes and the site category it belongs to. Announced investment counts a project as manufacturing when its NAICS code starts with 31, 32 or 33.

    Data sources

    • Bureau of Economic Analysis: GDP by state (1997–) and county (2001–), manufacturing and its industries.
    • Alabama Department of Commerce, New & Expanding Industry reports: announced projects, jobs and capital investment by county, 2016–.
    • USAspending.gov: federal contracts with manufacturers by place of performance, fiscal 2008–.
    • BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages: factory jobs and payroll.
    • BLS Consumer Price Index (FRED CPIAUCSL): used to put payroll in today's dollars.
    • us-atlas: county outlines from the Census Bureau.
