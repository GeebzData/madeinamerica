What each category includes

Every manufacturing industry BLS tracks sits in exactly one category and one industry group, so together they add up to all of manufacturing. The small numbers are NAICS industry codes, the federal system BLS uses to classify businesses.

Data sources

  • BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages: jobs by state and industry, private sector, annual averages.
  • BLS Current Employment Statistics: national manufacturing jobs since 1979 (via FRED).
  • Federal Reserve G.17 Industrial Production: manufacturing output (via FRED).
  • Census Bureau Construction Spending: private factory construction, adjusted with the BLS Producer Price Index for industrial building.
  • us-atlas: state outlines from the Census Bureau.
